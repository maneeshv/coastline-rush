# Coastline Rush v6 — Continuous Momentum

Portal Odyssey now uses one continuous expedition speed curve. Crossing a portal preserves the running pace, and the next world accelerates from that point. The old reset from the previous world's peak back to a slow starting speed is removed.

## Stage design

The opening of each world provides approximately three seconds of clear track at the retained speed. This gives the player time to read the changed environment and gravity. A pair of introductory encounters presents that world's movement rules, followed by its stronger encounter pool. Later worlds skip the long beginner section.

| World | Length | Standard speed | Expert speed | Starting encounter tier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| The Departure | 2,200m | 17 → 29m/s | 20 → 35m/s | Warm-up |
| Orbital Causeway | 3,100m | 29 → 33m/s | 35 → 39m/s | Pressure |
| Mars Outpost | 3,500m | 33 → 36m/s | 39 → 42m/s | Pressure |
| Verdant Nexus | 3,800m | 36 → 39m/s | 42 → 45m/s | Intense |
| Cryo Cathedral | 4,100m | 39 → 42m/s | 45 → 48m/s | Intense |
| The Star Forge | 4,400m | 42 → 45m/s | 48 → 51m/s | Overdrive |

Each row's finish speed is the next row's starting speed. Standard running speed reaches 162km/h; Expert reaches approximately 184km/h. Air Dash temporarily adds 6m/s and returns to the underlying curve when it expires. It does not become a permanent speed increase through a portal.

Later worlds are longer to preserve time in each environment despite the increased speed. Measured Standard stages last 98–102 seconds; the full route is about 10m13s including portals. Expert takes about 8m47s without abilities or revives. Starting a new run resets the expedition curve; selecting an unlocked later world starts at that world's intended speed.

## How difficulty grows

The worlds develop different skills at progressively higher speed:

1. **Harbor:** establish jump, slide, lane-change and cargo-roof timing.
2. **Orbit:** carry that momentum into long low-gravity leaps and broken decks.
3. **Mars:** add moving rover transfers, meteor lanes and landing choices.
4. **Verdant:** commit to root-jump and laser-slide combinations under sustained pressure.
5. **Cryo:** plan lanes before jumping because ice steering takes longer to settle.
6. **Forge:** start in Overdrive and sustain compound weaves, charging cargo, jumps and sentinel slides.

Local encounter tiers can rise further inside a world; the HUD names the current encounter pool. Its phase name may change when entering a world with different physics, while the expedition's base speed never falls. For example, Harbor's final Overdrive section leads into Orbit's Pressure pool at the same speed, with longer airborne decisions.

Speed and challenge increase without requiring every kind of obstacle to appear ever closer together. Orbit keeps enough time to complete a jump, and ice keeps its steering margin. Late worlds already begin at high encounter density, so their progression relies on retained acceleration and compound decisions rather than shortening recovery below physical limits. Clear portal approaches scale with speed, and the renderer reveals obstacles farther ahead at higher Odyssey speeds.

## Save compatibility

A saved v4 or v5 run finishes its current stage using the original geometry, speed and length, then adopts v6 at the next portal. The HUD marks such a current stage LEGACY. Start a new Odyssey to experience the full continuous curve immediately.

Wallets, unlocks, relics and earned medals remain intact. Timing and stage-score records from old routes are archived separately by course generation, including the distinct v4 and v5 records. Old Odyssey ghosts cannot race the changed course; original-mode ghosts remain compatible.
