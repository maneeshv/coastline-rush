# Coastline Rush v7 — Expedition Escapes

The expedition now has distinct endings, a route choice inside every world, run upgrades, focused practice, and an Endless Odyssey. Momentum still carries through portals. All additions use the native iOS and macOS game; nothing requires a server.

## Six authored finales

The final action sequence runs from 72% to 96% of each world, followed by the clear portal approach. Finale length varies with the current speed, generally around 20–30 seconds on the first expedition. Each sequence uses an authored ordering of collision patterns, with alternating lanes and the same jump/landing safety budgets as the main course.

| World | Finale | Setpiece and skill |
|---|---|---|
| Harbor | Harbor Lockdown | Cargo crane, moving freight, jump/slide combinations |
| Orbit | Collapsing Causeway | Breaking orbital structures and low-gravity gap decisions |
| Mars | Rover Convoy Escape | Large mining crawler, real moving rover roof transfers, meteor lanes |
| Verdant | The Living Gauntlet | Animated alien growth, root jumps and beam slides |
| Cryo | Glacier Fracture | Crystal/ice structures, fractured floor and slower lateral commitment |
| Forge | Sentinel Showdown | Large sentinel, reactor machinery and compound laser/cargo patterns |

The large scenery communicates the event. Collisions remain in the simulation's visible lane hazards; decorative scenery does not create invisible damage. This remains an original, stylized procedural art direction, with richer courier armor, integrated stride, steering lean, takeoff and landing weight.

## Branches inside the run

Choose during the marked 20–26% window, using the route buttons or steering left/right. The route locks at 26%, divides during 32–55%, and rejoins before the finale. Left defaults to the safer route; right selects a denser elevated route with double-value coins. The decks, player, obstacles and collectibles share the same physical path transform.

| World | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| Harbor | Tidal channel: real Jet Ski handling, gates, wakes, traffic and tricks | Cargo rooftops |
| Orbit | Service causeway | Outer ring |
| Mars | Crater floor | Rover convoy |
| Verdant | Forest floor | Canopy bridge |
| Cryo | Glacier shelf | Crystal ridge |
| Forge | Service conduit | Reactor gantry |

Harbor water controls change with the vehicle: drag to steer, down to brake, up after a wake for a trick, and B/double-tap/Boost for speed. The water segment returns to running automatically. The standalone Wave Rider race remains available.

## Upgrade tradeoffs

After crossing a portal, open Loadout during the clear arrival window to pause and choose one available upgrade. Choices last for the run and are saved with checkpoints. They are optional, and each can be chosen once.

- **Extended Phase:** Phase Step lasts 1.8 seconds instead of 1.2; it costs two charges.
- **Dash Dividend:** Air Dash lasts 0.8 seconds and clean escapes earn an extra 75 points during its reward window; it costs two charges.
- **Relic Capacitor:** store five charges instead of three, but recover a charge once per two relics.

Daily Odyssey fixes the route and upgrade order. Campaign ghosts replay recorded route/perk decisions and the equipped artifact; choices in later worlds beyond a partial ghost resume normally. Changing artifacts after starting a normal run stops ghost recording for that attempt, because the old recording could no longer reproduce that loadout. Artifacts stay fixed while racing the recorded portion of a ghost.

## Practice, Endless and skill contracts

Campaign crosses the sixth portal to finish. Endless Odyssey wraps back to Harbor, keeps accelerating across cycles, and uses a bounded base-speed ceiling of 65m/s Standard or 71m/s Expert. Later cycles use Overdrive encounter pressure with physical recovery margins. Endless scores and world records are stored separately from Campaign; Endless does not award a finite expedition completion.

The Atlas starts an unlocked world's final ~30 seconds in Practice. Practice never changes coins, keys, boards, unlocks, records, medals, missions or skill contracts. Practice retries do not spend keys. Practice and Endless do not offer personal ghosts.

Five skill contracts reward distinct achievements once: three different risky worlds, a clean unprotected Orbit with eight skill actions, all six clean finales with two skill actions each, four actual Mars rover rides in a finished stage, and three completed local objectives in one stage. Rewards and progress survive restarts without duplicate payouts.

## Readability and replay

Jump, slide and dodge hazards have distinct shape cues and short action sounds. High Contrast strengthens their backing and outlines. Reduced Motion respects both the game setting and the operating system preference; it removes camera shake, bob, FOV warp and flashing effects while retaining essential movement cues.

After a crash, **See what happened** replays a bounded buffer of the last approximately three seconds. Drag the timeline to inspect the collision. The live simulation, wallet and score stay frozen. The buffer is ephemeral and is not restored after quitting the app.

## Performance and compatibility

Settings → Device diagnostics shows measured local callback cadence, p95/p99 callback intervals, CPU scene-update time, input-received-to-next-tick timing, quality and thermal state. These are not GPU presentation, physical touch-to-photon or battery measurements. Data stays local. Thermal/low-power conditions reduce render quality, and world/entity caches remain bounded.

Saved v4, v5 and v6 runs retain their exact current-stage rules and adopt v7 after the next portal. Existing wallets and unlocks remain. Prior route records are archived; incompatible old Odyssey ghosts are excluded, while original-mode ghosts remain compatible.
