# Coastline Rush v4 — World Mastery

This update adds gameplay depth, original articulated character work, richer surfaces, replay goals and rendering controls across the existing native iOS/macOS game. All gameplay and records work offline.

## Every world asks for a different skill

| World | Required and optional encounters |
|---|---|
| The Departure | Full-width jump and slide phrases teach the action vocabulary; optional roof relic routes reward commitment. |
| Orbital Causeway | Broken decks require long low-gravity jumps; aerial coin arcs mark the trajectory. |
| Mars Outpost | Meteor impact lanes, moving rover/ramp pairs, elevated roof pickups and barrier jumps. |
| Verdant Nexus | Alternating root jumps and low energy sweeps demand jump/slide combinations. |
| Cryo Cathedral | Cracked ice jumps, low arches and slower steering require earlier lane commitments. |
| The Star Forge | Repeated sentinel sweeps and charging cargo create the final pressure sequence. This is a hazard encounter, not a separate enemy AI or combat boss. |

Each world retains a safe opening and clear portal approach. Distances and speeds remain bounded. The seeded layouts vary while retaining physically possible action timing. Relics are optional and do not lock the portal.

## Movement and portals

- Up/Space/W or an upward swipe jumps; Down/S or a downward swipe rolls and fast-falls.
- A jump input within 120ms of landing is buffered. Chained swipes no longer require lifting the finger between every direction; sensitivity is adjustable.
- The courier has an original procedural joint rig with shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, ankles and head, with blended running, airborne, rolling and landing poses.
- Landing compression, rings, sparks and reusable iPhone haptic generators reinforce contact. Physical-device haptic feel is not yet verified.
- Jump in the final 14m to earn precision portal credit. The protected transition maintains visual forward motion and combo momentum.
- Portal assist carries the runner through automatically. Turn it off for an explicit jump; the runner waits safely if the jump is missed.
- Opening the loadout chooser pauses gameplay. Close it and resume when ready.

## Artifacts and mastery

Choose an ability in the Star Atlas, or use Loadout in a safe landing/portal zone. Recovering a relic grants a charge, capped at three. Tap the artifact button or press **E** to activate.

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| Air Dash | A short forward burst and lift; overhead hazards still matter. |
| Phase Step | 1.2 seconds of timed hazard protection. |
| Aegis Shield | Arms one impact absorption. An absorbed hit still disqualifies a clean-stage medal. |

Bronze requires completing the stage. Silver requires zero impacts and at least two clean obstacle actions. Gold adds at least two relics and a precision portal entry. The highest medal, best stage score and best clear time persist; later weaker runs cannot erase mastery. Late-world practice remains separate from a full six-world expedition.

## Jet Ski handling

Drag sideways for continuous steering or use keyboard lane targets. The craft has lateral inertia and changing currents; braking reduces drift. Gates still govern the timer, while moving traffic makes early turns risky. Wake ramps enable up/left/right trick combinations. Trick points remain pending until a controlled landing; unstable landings lose the pending bonus. Boost remains a charge-based speed decision, without collision immunity.

## Daily Odyssey and personal ghosts

Daily Odyssey uses one deterministic six-world seed per UTC date, always beginning at the harbor on Standard. Its daily record is separate from legacy daily Endless records.

Completed attempts with enough movement save a ghost. **Race Your Ghost** in the Atlas or Settings restarts its exact seed, difficulty and starting world. Daily retries automatically find the matching recording. Ghosts are translucent, noncolliding opponents, never blended across unrelated worlds. Recording pauses with the game and survives checkpoints. A better attempt replaces the previous recording for that course.

Storage is bounded to eight courses and 3,600 samples per course. Sampling starts at 10Hz; long runs reduce sample density. Old generator revisions and incompatible courses are excluded. Malformed optional replay data cannot discard the actual active run.

## Rendering and resources

- Native renderer callbacks drive updates through a bounded main-thread relay; there is no competing 60Hz simulation timer.
- SwiftUI HUD state publishes at up to 20Hz during play, with immediate action/phase updates.
- Paused/background scenes stop continuous animation; menus request 30fps and gameplay requests 60fps. A request is not a measured device frame rate.
- Hidden harbor animation work is skipped in other worlds.
- The renderer incrementally prepares the next destination and retains only the active and next world. Completed geometry is submitted to SceneKit's asynchronous GPU resource preparation API.
- Entity pools remain bounded and survive destination changes.
- Automatic/Fast/Balanced/Ultra quality adjusts shadows, ambient occlusion, bloom, particles, visibility distance and antialiasing. Low Power Mode or serious/critical thermal state selects the reduced-cost preset.
- Mars has irregular layered terrain meshes with normals/UVs and an original generated rock texture. Other decks, hazards and materials have distinct world treatments.

This is stylized native 3D with original procedural meshes, not a licensed AAA character/animation library or photorealistic production. There is no new game engine, ray tracing, online leaderboard or cloud save service.

## Original texture provenance

`Resources/MarsRock-v4.png` was generated using the built-in image-generation tool, opaque, with no reference image. The selected output was copied into this project and is loaded as a cached canyon/boulder albedo. Exact prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square albedo texture for a native 3D mobile sci-fi game's Martian sandstone canyon meshes. Generate one flat orthographic close-up of rich rust-red eroded sandstone rock, fine sedimentary bands, irregular natural fractures, subtle dusty orange and deep brown mineral variation, realistic small granular pores and restrained medium-scale erosion. Uniform diffuse ambient illumination with no directional baked shadows, no horizon, no perspective, no objects, no scene, no sky, no text, no symbols, no borders. Edge-to-edge surface, tileable texture feeling. Material should read at both near and far distances with medium contrast, avoiding repeated geometric triangles. 2048 square production texture, opaque.

The delivered texture is 1,254 × 1,254 pixels; 2,048 in the prompt was the requested art direction, not a claimed output size.
