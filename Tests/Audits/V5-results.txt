The density probe removes approaching hazards solely to inspect the generated distribution without deaths; survival audits never replace the track. Orbit intentionally retains enough time to finish the long low-gravity arc. Expert has faster speeds and shorter encounter intervals; hazard counts vary with randomized compound patterns.

Solver issues fixed during verification:
1. A moving crate can pull ahead of its neighboring beam. Read the next obstacle in the actual occupied/target lane, even while an earlier off-lane collider passes.
2. Cryo landing combinations require reading the visible coin arc before jumping; waiting for landing obstacles to become the first row leads to late two-lane corrections.
No production cadence or obstacle edits were made to pass these solver fixes.

Limitations:
- This verifies deterministic simulated feasibility, not human difficulty calibration or real-device input latency, sustained FPS, thermals or battery life.
- The controller reads already-spawned visible geometry and coin markers; it does not inspect RNG or unrevealed generator rows. It makes frame-by-frame decisions and therefore models skilled idealized play.
- JourneyTests and RouteAudit share the same action controller; these are overlapping checks, not independent player strategies.
- Safe lane-only rejection is tested separately without jump, slide, boards or artifacts.
- Wallet/profile migration and native builds are validated by the root task separately.
